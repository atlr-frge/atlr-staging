Privacy

What this site collects.

Nothing beyond the server log, because this site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, loads nothing from a third party and serves its own fonts from this domain, so there is no tracking script here for a browser to block or for us to answer for.

Visiting

atlr.pro is a set of static pages served by GitHub Pages, which records the requests it serves under GitHub’s privacy statement, and we see no visitor data from it. The contact form posts what you type to FormSubmit, which relays it to hello@atlr.pro and keeps no copy for us to read later.

Writing to us

Email to hello@atlr.pro reaches the two founders through Google Workspace and is read by them alone, kept for as long as the conversation or engagement it belongs to, and deleted on request to the same address. We do not add you to a list, and we do not share your address with anyone.

Client material

Material shared for a Sprint or an engagement stays under the confidentiality terms agreed for it. Personal information is tokenised before any model call, routing is restricted in code to approved regions, and inputs are hash-checked against their source. The five controls on the Work page describe how.

Questions

For anything about this page, or to ask what we hold about you, write to hello@atlr.pro at Atelier Superintelligence Inc., Toronto, Canada.
