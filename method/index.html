Method · Forge

Learn, build, prove, stay.

Four moves, in order, every time. The Sprint comes first, so you see something working on your own workflow before you commit to anything else.

00
The Forge Sprint

One week, one system, built on your workflow and connected to the systems you name, with any connection still awaiting access stubbed and marked, and synthetic data wherever real data cannot be used yet. By Friday it runs end to end in a demo environment and you watch it work, at a fixed scope and a fixed price agreed before Monday.

If it is right, we go further. If it is not, you have lost a week rather than a year.

01
Learn

Time inside the domain first: the workflow as it actually moves, the data and where it lives, and the people who decide. We talk to the team, map the systems, and find the constraints nobody else asks about. Because we arrive from outside your field, the questions we ask are the ones your team stopped asking years ago.

Two to four weeks of embedded discovery before we scope anything larger than the Sprint.

02
Build

On the real workflow, inside your cloud, on the models you approve, with synthetic data wherever real data cannot be used yet. A synthetic population lets a prototype exist before data access is approved, and the scope names what runs and what is still to be built.

You see it running on your own data before we move past this stage.

03
Prove

Working end to end in a demo environment, behind automated tests, before production. The system is measured against the number you care about and against your own records, and go-live is gated on a run against your own records, with the miss rate reported to the person who owns the outcome.

Every engagement ships an evidence pack for whoever owns model risk.

04
Stay

Maintained after hand-off and improved from what your reviewers accept and reject. The system takes on more of your workflow and more of your stack each month; the compliance platform’s build has grown from an agent set to filing validation to a KYC module since 2025.

Scope grows from what the reviewers accept, and it is priced per addition.

The Sprint, in detail

What a week produces.

What you see on Friday

One workflow running end to end in a demo environment on our cloud: your documents and data shapes loaded as context, the agents drafting their outputs with evidence attached, a review queue where your people accept or reject what comes through, and a record of every call. We measure it against the baseline you gave us on Monday and show you both numbers.

Monday brief and baseline → Tuesday to Thursday build → Friday you watch it run

What we need from you

A workflow you would like to see run, the people who decide on it, the shape of the data it touches (synthetic is fine for the week), the systems it has to talk to, and the number you would measure it by. Half a day of your team’s time on Monday and an hour on Friday is the cost to you.

Fixed scope · fixed price · agreed before the week starts
