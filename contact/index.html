Start with a Sprint.

One week, one system, on your workflow, at a fixed scope and a fixed price. Write to hello@atlr.pro with the workflow you have in mind and we reply within one business day.

What a Sprint needs from you
  • A workflow you would like to see run, ideally the one that eats the most skilled hours each week, because that is where the first agent earns its place.
  • The people who decide on it today, half a day of their time on Monday and an hour on Friday.
  • The shape of the data it touches. Synthetic data is fine for the week; a prototype can exist before data access is approved.
  • The systems it has to talk to, and whose cloud it should run in.
  • The number you would measure it by, taken at the brief, so Friday has a baseline.

If you have two of the five, write anyway. The first call is where the other three get worked out.

Before the brief we sign your NDA, or ours. The Sprint runs on synthetic data unless you choose otherwise, and client material stays under the confidentiality terms of the engagement. The questions a security or model-risk team asks are answered on the Trust page.

Two other ways we work.

Not every engagement is a Sprint sold to the company that will run the system. Two other shapes are already in use.

Inside your product

For a software platform, we build the agent layer inside your product: in your repository, behind your tests, released under your name. The KYC module on the Work page was delivered this way, as pull requests against the platform’s own codebase, with the pipeline running under health probes until the platform took it over.

Behind your engagement

For a consultancy or an agency, we are the build team behind your client work. You hold the relationship and the paper, we bring the context engineering, the integrations and the five controls, and the evidence pack ships under your engagement.
