On every job

Five controls, every build.

The same controls run in the financial-crime work, where the approvals and the record are what a regulator examines, and in production systems for broadcast and music.

01Validation before use

Agents and models are measured against the institution’s own records, or a production’s own reference material, before either is relied on. Fourteen upscalers were scored against a 4K reference on a network series this autumn, and the plain resize came closest and became the default.

02Human approval

The analyst decides every case and the compliance officer approves every report before it is filed. In production work a person accepts or rejects every generated take, and an archive agent’s drafts wait on a review page until someone has seen them.

03Audit record

Every model call and human decision is written to a hash-chained record. Each generated take keeps its model, prompt and settings, and its cost where the provider reports it.

04Release control

Every release passes automated tests and a health check before it rolls out. Each delivery is cut to a new numbered folder by a script that refuses to overwrite, with a checksum on every copy.

05Data protection

Personal information is tokenised before any model sees it, and model routing is restricted in code to approved regions. Client material stays under each production’s confidentiality terms, and review sites decrypt only in the viewer’s browser.

The Forge Sprint

Before any long engagement we run a one-week Sprint: one system, built on your workflow and connected to your infrastructure, with synthetic data wherever real data cannot be used yet. You see something working before you commit to anything else, at a fixed scope and a fixed price.

This is how most engagements begin.

01
Learn

Time inside the domain first: the workflow as it actually moves, the data and where it lives, and the people who decide. We talk to the team, map the systems, and find the constraints nobody else asks about.

Two to four weeks of embedded discovery before we scope anything larger than the Sprint.

02
Build

On the real workflow, inside your cloud, on the models you approve, with synthetic data wherever real data cannot be used yet. The scope is honest about what works and what does not yet.

You see it running on your own data before we move past this stage.

03
Prove

Working end to end in a demo environment, behind automated tests, before production. The system is measured against the number you care about and against your own records, and the miss rate goes to the person who owns the outcome.

Every engagement ships an evidence pack for whoever owns model risk.

04
Stay

Maintained after hand-off and improved from what your reviewers accept and reject. The system takes on more of your workflow and more of your stack every month, and what you have in month twelve is a different order of magnitude from month one.

Most clients expand scope within the first two quarters.
